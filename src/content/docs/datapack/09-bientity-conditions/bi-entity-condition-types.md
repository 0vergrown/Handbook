title: "Bi-entity Condition Types"
description: "Bi-entity Condition Types operate on a Pair<Entity, Entity>, or in simpler terms: an actor and a target."
---

Bi-entity Condition Types operate on a `Pair<Entity, Entity>`, or in simpler terms: an actor and a target. The actor and target are determined by the used power type, and can be swapped. These are available to power/condition types that provide a `bientity_condition` object field.
